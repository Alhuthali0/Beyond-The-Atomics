SMTP Setup Guide

To enable email reporting, configure your mail provider's SMTP details. Modern providers usually require an App Password instead of your primary password.

  1. Locate your provider's SMTP settings (e.g., smtp.gmail.com for Google, smtp.office365.com for Microsoft).
  2. Ensure 2-Step Verification (2FA) is enabled on your email account.
  3. Navigate to your account's security settings and generate a unique App Password or API Key.
  4. Enter the SMTP Server address and Port (commonly 587 for TLS).
  5. Input your email address as the Username and the generated App Password as the Password.
  6. Click Test Connection to verify settings before saving.
